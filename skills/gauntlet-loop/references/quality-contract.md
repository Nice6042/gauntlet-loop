# Quality contract

## Absolute Wowed
Every applicable hard-gated metric is NO_IDENTIFIED_ROOM_FOR_IMPROVEMENT within the frozen scope and evidence boundary. Persistent and fresh blind critics must approve. Any configured multi-critic agreement rule must also be met.

## Strict Wowed
No material actionable gap remains. Critical/high and meaningful medium findings are zero, required tests pass, and production hard gates are satisfied.

## User-Defined
The owner freezes per-metric thresholds, hard gates, residual-finding allowances, critic agreement, evidence requirements, and resource limits before execution.

## Main-Agent Recommended
The Main Agent proposes a risk-based mixed contract for explicit owner approval.

## Bug Hunt additive gates

Bug Hunt closure also requires frozen area/search coverage to be accounted for;
every candidate to have an evidence-bearing terminal status; every implemented
fix to pass original reproduction, focused regression, fresh Fix Verifier, and
integration review; and every unresolved specification or blocked fix to remain
disclosed. Queue emptiness, one discovery pass, or `AREA_NO_VERIFIED_BUGS` does
not prove the absence of bugs.

Resource exhaustion never creates success. Use truthful terminal states such as BUDGET_EXHAUSTED_NOT_WOWED, ITERATION_LIMIT_REACHED_NOT_WOWED, BLOCKED_BY_CAPABILITY, BLOCKED_BY_PERMISSION, or BLOCKED_BY_DEPENDENCY.
